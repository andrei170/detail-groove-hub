VSL Outline (3-5 minute cohort pitch)

This VSL is written for the proposed "Detail Groove Business Cohort" product. It assumes the cohort exists. Swap-slot pricing and seat cap per final offer design at kickoff.

HOOK (0:00-0:20)

"You already know how to detail. That is not the problem. The problem is that knowing how to detail has nothing to do with running a detailing business."

[On screen: the number $20K/mo. Cut to a detailer in a parking lot working alone. Cut back to the number.]

FRAME THE PROBLEM (0:20-1:00)

"Most detailers hit the same wall. You are good at the work. You have some clients. You are doing $3K, $5K, maybe $8K a month. And you are working as hard as you can to stay there.

Here is what is actually happening. Your prices are not right for the hours you are putting in. You do not have a system to get clients consistently - you are relying on referrals and hoping the phone rings. And every time you get busy enough to think about hiring, you do not know what to pay someone or how to make sure the quality stays up.

That is not a skill problem. That is an infrastructure problem. And no YouTube video, no matter how good, is going to build that infrastructure for you."

CREDIBILITY (1:00-1:30)

"Detail Groove has been in this niche since 2011. The YouTube channel has 252,000 subscribers. The Detailing Business Class podcast has thousands of listeners every week. The free content works - it is why you are watching this.

The content tells you what to look for. The cohort is where you actually build it."

THE OFFER (1:30-2:30)

"The Detail Groove Business Cohort is an 8-week program for detailers who are past the free content and not yet at $20K/mo.

Here is what gets installed by the end:

Week 1-2: Pricing for profitability. Not general pricing theory. Your specific job types, your specific hours, your specific market. By the end of week 2 your pricing is fixed.

Week 3-4: Client acquisition system. Not 'post more content.' A repeatable system for getting the right clients on a schedule you control.

Week 5-6: Systems and SOPs. The operational infrastructure that lets you deliver consistent quality without you physically doing every job.

Week 7-8: Your first hire. Who to hire, how to find them, how to train them, and how to make the math work.

[SWAP-SLOT: insert cohort pricing] per person. [SWAP-SLOT: seat cap] seats per cohort. One group call per week.

This is not another course library. You will not log into a video portal and get 40 lessons you never finish. Every week there is a live call, a specific implementation assignment, and a check-in before we move to the next module."

"We have been teaching the business side of detailing for 10+ years. The operators who have gone through the Houston workshops and the high-level coaching have crossed $20K/mo and kept going.

The cohort is the version of that for operators who are not ready for the in-person or the 1:1 yet - but who are past where the free content can take them.
