The script (section by section)

Section 1 - Hook (0:00-0:25)

"If you found this site because you saw one of my videos, here is something I should tell you up front.

The YouTube channel has hundreds of thousands of subscribers. The Instagram account has tens of thousands more. The podcast has been running since 2020.

The storefront under it has not had a video on the homepage. Until today.

5 minutes. Tells you what to do next, based on where you actually are."

Section 2 - The ladder (0:25-1:30)

"Detail Groove sits on top of a real operating shop. R3 Auto Detailing. Houston. Since 2011. That is the credibility behind the courses, the podcast, the in-person training.

Every operator who builds a detailing business goes through the same 4 bands.

Band 1: Just Starting. Cleaning friends' cars in the driveway. No pricing. No marketing. The bottleneck is getting the first $1,000 in the door.

Band 2: Building To $1K. You can clean cars. You cannot predictably book them. The bottleneck is the marketing surface and the pricing model.

Band 3: Building To $5K. You can clean. You can book. The bottleneck is systems. Route density. Pricing tiers. The first hire.

Band 4: Building To $20K. You are running a real shop. The bottleneck is the operator step above. Not more effort. More structure."

Section 3 - The catalog map (1:30-2:45)

"Here is what Detail Groove has at each band.

Just Starting: the free guide and the Rapid Detailing Business Traction course. Goal: your first $1,000 in 60 days or less.

Building To $1K: Detailing as a Business (pricing + operations) and Social Media For Detailers (the marketing surface). Run them together.

Building To $5K: the Detailers Growth Academy. All-access. Every course I publish. The coaching cadence.

Building To $20K: this is the band where the audience asked for something I had not yet built.

Above the Detailers Growth Academy is the new Detailer Operator Cohort. Application-gated. 8-12 operators per cycle. 2 days in-person at the R3 shop right behind me. 8-12 week implementation runway after.

The cohort is the room. The Detailers Growth Academy is the catalog. The 2-Day In-Person training is the tactical week. The free guide is the front door."

Section 4 - The scorecard frame (2:45-4:00)

"If you watched a YouTube video and landed here, the question is: which one fits where you actually are?

The scorecard answers that in 90 seconds. 10 questions. Tells you which band you are in. Tells you which course or cohort fits the band. Tells you the single biggest bottleneck you are about to hit.

The link is right under this video. Take the scorecard before you browse the courses.

The reason: I have watched operators buy the wrong course at the wrong band for years. They do not finish it. They quit. They blame the course. The course was right for someone, just not for them at that moment.

The scorecard fixes that."

Section 5 - The cohort frame (4:00-5:15)

"If you are already past $5K and the audience for this video is mostly past $5K, the cohort is what to look at.

8-12 operators per cycle. Application is 4 minutes. Manual review by me within 48 hours.

2 days here at the R3 shop. Morning open from the bay. The tool wall. The wash bay. The job board. The customer flow. You see it. You walk it. We trade live in your operation while the cohort is in the room.

8-12 weeks after the 2 days. Weekly group call. Office hours twice a month. The journal cadence.

The cohort is not a course. The cohort is the operator step above the course.

The application asks the questions that calibrate the fit. The application page surfaces the cycle pricing on submit. The cycle pricing is above the Detailers Growth Academy line. That is the only number I am saying on camera. The rest is on the application."

Section 6 - Close (5:15-6:00)

"Two buttons under this video.

Button 1: take the 90-second scorecard. Find your band. Get the routing.

Button 2: browse the courses if you already know what you are looking for.

If you are at Band 4 and the cohort sounds like the fit, the apply link is on the scorecard result page.

The work is the same one I have been doing since 2011. The funnel under it is finally caught up to the audience.

Take the scorecard."
