Detail Groove | 30-Day Content Strategy

Slug: detail-groove Purpose: 30-day content calendar for the detailer-business-coach register Register: Operator-to-operator. Business-first. Zero-to-$20K/mo framing. Platforms: YouTube (primary authority), Instagram (distribution), TikTok (reach), Podcast (depth) Prepared: 2026-05-16

Content Pillars (4 recurring themes)

Pillar What It Covers Register
A - The Business Math Pricing, margins, hourly rate, job profitability Specific numbers. Always.
B - Client Acquisition How to get clients, repeat business, referral systems System-led, not hustle-led
C - Operations + Hiring SOPs, first hire, quality control without being on-site Infrastructure over inspiration
D - The $20K/mo Benchmark What operators at $20K/mo do differently than operators at $5K/mo Benchmark-specific

Ratio: A (30%) / B (30%) / C (20%) / D (20%)

Content Rules

  1. Every YouTube video ends with the quiz link until the quiz is live. After cohort launches, alternate: quiz (top-of-funnel episodes) / cohort waitlist (implementation-heavy episodes).

  2. Every podcast episode adds the 45-second end-card as of the first episode post-install. No exceptions. Retro-apply to top 10 episodes by download volume.

  3. Instagram carousels always end with one CTA slide. One link only (quiz or cohort waitlist). Do not stack multiple CTAs on one post.

  4. TikTok always routes to YouTube or bio link. TikTok is reach, not conversion. Do not build complex CTAs on TikTok.

  5. No technique-only content in this calendar. Every piece must address a business problem (pricing, client acquisition, hiring, operations, revenue target) not a craft problem (polish technique, product selection, paint correction method). Technique content is Detail Groove's existing library - do not dilute the business-coach brand with it.

  6. All numbers in content are operator-specific. Not "thousands of dollars" - "$150 per job," "$5K/mo plateau," "$20K/mo benchmark." If a number cannot be stated precisely, do not use it.

  7. No banned phrases. Universal AG1 list applies plus Detail Groove-specific ban list from copy-strategy.md.
