Phase 3

Detail Groove | Content Strategy

Prepared: 2026-05-17 Pitch type: Cold first-touch Scope: How the existing Detail Groove content surfaces route into the four-piece patch. No new content load on Oscar.

Anchor principle

The four-piece patch (VSL + scorecard + cohort + segmented welcome) does not require any new YouTube videos, IG reels, podcast episodes, or shorts. The existing content surfaces all route to the scorecard. The scorecard does the segmentation. The segmentation routes to the right tier in the existing catalog or to the new cohort.

1. YouTube routing (the highest-leverage surface)

Element Current After install
End cards on every long-form video Either subscribe button or generic link to detailgroove.co Route 100% of end cards to the scorecard landing
Channel description link detailgroove.co homepage (PDF lead magnet) Move primary link to the scorecard landing. Keep the PDF as the secondary link.
Pinned comment on top 10 most-viewed videos Mixed / none verified Pin: "If you're building a detailing business, 90 seconds to figure out which tier you're at: [scorecard URL]"
Community tab posts Periodic One post per week routing to the scorecard
Shorts (if active) Direct to channel Description routes to scorecard
Description boilerplate on new uploads Whatever Oscar writes Add 1 line: "Scorecard for where you're at in your detailing business: [URL]"

Why this works. The YouTube channel is the largest single funnel surface. Routing every end card, every description, every community post into the scorecard captures email at scale for the first time and segments at capture-time.

2. Instagram routing

Element Current After install
Bio link Linktree or single CTA Switch to the scorecard landing. Linktree backup link to /courses for course shoppers who know what they want.
Reel captions Mix of CTAs Standard CTA on every business-side reel: "Bio link runs the 90-sec scorecard. Tells you which tier you're at."
Story CTAs (weekly) Variable One story per week routes to the scorecard.
DM auto-reply (ManyChat or equivalent) If active, unknown Set a keyword: "SCORE" returns the scorecard link in DM.

3. Podcast routing (The Detailing Business Class)

Element Current After install
Episode intro / outro Standard Add 15-sec read on the scorecard at the top of every new episode. "Take 90 seconds. Find out where you're at. Link in the show notes."
Show notes (every episode) Standard Top of show notes: scorecard link.
Buzzsprout / Apple / Spotify podcast description boilerplate Standard Update boilerplate: scorecard link as the first line.
Guest episodes (if Oscar's the host) Standard Add 15-sec mid-roll: scorecard link.

Why this works. Podcast listeners are deeper-funnel than channel viewers. The scorecard captures them at the highest-intent moment.

4. TikTok routing (@detailgrooveco)

Element Current After install

Caption CTA Mix Same as IG: standard CTA on every business-side post.

5. Facebook group (Detail Groove Training)

Element Current After install

Pinned post Whatever current Pin: scorecard + cohort application invitation
Weekly post cadence Whatever current One scorecard-routed post per week, evergreen

6. X (Twitter) routing

Element Current After install

7. Lead magnet (existing PDF)

The "Ultimate Guide To Start Your Detailing Business" PDF stays as-is. Two changes:

  1. The PDF download confirmation page adds the scorecard as a second CTA: "You'll get the guide in your inbox in 2 minutes. While you wait - take the 90-sec scorecard to find which course matches where you're at."
  2. The PDF nurture sequence (Day 1, 3, 7) adds the scorecard CTA in the P.S. of email 1 and email 3.

8. The new piece - one-time launch content

The patch DOES require one-time launch content for the cohort. Three pieces, all leveraging existing surfaces:

  1. One YouTube video. Title: "The Detailer Operator Cohort - Why I'm Launching It." 6-9 min, filmed at the R3 shop. Explains the gap between the all-access and the cohort. Routes to the cohort landing.
  2. Two podcast episodes. Solo episodes, ~30 min each. Episode 1: "Past the All-Access - The Cohort Is The Next Step." Episode 2: "Inside The 2 Days At R3 - What The Cohort Actually Looks Like."
  3. One launch IG / TikTok cycle. 5 reels over 10 days, each pulling 1 segment from the YouTube launch video. Routes to the cohort landing.

Total new content load: 1 YouTube video + 2 podcast episodes + 5 reels. Oscar films the YouTube + records the podcasts. AG1 cuts the reels from the YouTube video and writes the captions.

9. Cadence (post-install, evergreen)

Surface Cadence Action
YouTube Every new upload End card routes to scorecard. Description includes scorecard link.
Podcast Every new episode 15-sec read + show notes scorecard link.
IG 1 story per week Routes to scorecard
FB group 1 pinned post per week Scorecard + cohort invitation, rotating
Lead magnet PDF Every new download Confirmation page + nurture sequence include scorecard CTA

10. What this strategy explicitly does NOT do

  • Does not change the content register on any existing surface
  • Does not require a new video, reel, or post on Day 1 except the cohort-launch trio
  • Does not ask Oscar to film any AG1-built sales content. AG1 builds the scorecard, the landing, the cohort surface. The content stays Oscar's.
  • Does not consolidate or kill any existing channel. All four surfaces (YT + IG + TikTok + podcast) stay live and active.

Disclaimer

Content-routing cadence above is suggested based on current public surface. Final cadence locks with Oscar on Day 1 of any install. The strategy stays at the level of where to route CTAs, not what content to produce. Detail Groove's content register is Oscar's, not AG1's.
