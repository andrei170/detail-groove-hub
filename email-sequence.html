E7 - Day 14 | The Workshop Ceiling

Subject: The $1,200 workshop is a strong product. 4 students is the ceiling.

Hey,

The in-person Houston workshop is a strong product. 2 days, hands-on, small group, real curriculum. The operators who attend that workshop get something the courses cannot deliver: live reps, live feedback, live operator-to-operator problem solving.

The cap is structural: 4 students per class. Houston location required. An operator in Atlanta, Toronto, or London cannot attend.

Two ways to break that ceiling:

Option 1 - Online Intensive ($800-$1,000): The workshop curriculum delivered digitally. Pre-recorded technique modules + live Q&A sessions + implementation calls. Removes the geography constraint. Adds the "Upgrade to In-Person" upsell for operators who want the Houston experience after completing the online version. 50 students/year at $900 = $45K direct. Houston waitlist fills from online grads.

Option 2 - Regional Licensing: License the workshop curriculum to verified operators in other markets. Each licensee runs their own 4-person workshop, pays a per-cohort licensing fee. Scales the in-person footprint without adding Detail Groove locations. $1,000/cohort x 10 regional trainers x 4 cohorts/year = $40K in licensing fees. Plus Detail Groove gets verified operators in new markets as distribution partners.

I can build either. Or both. The infrastructure for a licensing system is more complex but the long-term margin is higher.
