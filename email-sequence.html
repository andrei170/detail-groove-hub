Voice rules across all 4 sequences

  • Signed by Oscar (or "Detail Groove team" for post-application transactional)
  • Operator-to-operator register
  • No banned phrases (per copy-strategy)
  • No em-dashes anywhere
  • Short paragraphs
  • One CTA per email (with one optional P.S. CTA)
  • No income guarantees
  • Cohort framing stays as operator-discipline, not P&L
