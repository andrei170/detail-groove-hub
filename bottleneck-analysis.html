Detail Groove | Bottleneck Analysis

Prepared: 2026-05-17 Pitch type: Cold first-touch Method: Four-piece structural map. One sentence on each gap, the fix, year-1 floor math, what AG1 installs.

The one-sentence read

Detail Groove has ~249K YouTube subscribers, ~83K Instagram followers, ~15 years of Oscar Gil's operating credibility through R3 Auto Detailing, a 6-course catalog, a $1,200 in-person training, and a podcast active since 2020. The funnel underneath that audience is a free PDF opt-in on the homepage, six course cards side by side with no filter, and a $1,200 Houston-only in-person SKU. The four-piece patch sits on top of the existing infrastructure without rebuilding any course, any podcast, or any sister-shop surface.

Bottleneck 1 - No pre-sell VSL on detailgroove.co

What is observable. The homepage above-the-fold is a PDF lead magnet opt-in. No 5-7 minute pre-sell video. The mass YouTube and Instagram audience routes onto a static product card and a free PDF promise.

Why it matters. Roughly 249K subscribers who already trust the on-camera voice get a register switch the moment they land on the storefront. Operator-educator on YouTube. Lead-magnet capture on the storefront. The pre-sell never carries over.

The fix. Film a 5-7 minute homepage VSL at the R3 Auto Detailing shop in Oscar's operator-educator voice. Script the ladder ($0 -> $1K -> $5K -> $20K), pre-sell the all-access Detailers Growth Academy, route too-early viewers to the lead magnet, route ready-to-buy viewers to the right course. Embed above the lead-magnet opt-in.

Year-1 floor math. Conservative VSL-driven conversion lift on the existing storefront traffic alone (no extra ads, no new audience): low six figures incremental across the existing course catalog and the in-person SKU. Compounds against every YouTube end-card click, every IG link-in-bio click, every podcast show-notes click.

AG1 service. VSL scripting. Direct the on-shop record. Edit the cut. Embed on the homepage. Caption pass.

Bottleneck 2 - No scorecard between visitor and the 6-course catalog

What is observable. The /courses page lists 6+ course cards side by side. SEO For Detailers. Detailing as a Business. Social Media For Detailers. No Talk Just Action. Smoke Odor Removal. Six Figure Email Templates. Plus the Detailers Growth Academy all-access. No filter step between visitor and the right SKU.

Why it matters. Choice paralysis kills checkout. A new visitor cannot self-identify which course matches their revenue band, their solo-vs-team stage, or their primary bottleneck. The natural default becomes the lowest-friction click, not the highest-fit purchase. Pricing transparency on the Social Media For Detailers course ($297 one-time or 4 x $97) sets the expectation that other courses sit in the same band - but the buyer cannot tell which one fits without reading every page.

The fix. 10-question Detailer Readiness Scorecard. Returns one of four bands: Just Starting (route to free guide + Rapid Detailing Business Traction), Building To $1K (route to Detailing as a Business + Social Media For Detailers), Building To $5K (route to Detailers Growth Academy all-access), Building To $20K (route to the new application-gated cohort + the in-person training). Captures email. Tags by service line (mobile vs ceramic vs paint correction vs interior), revenue band, employee count, and primary bottleneck.

Year-1 floor math. Segmented capture across the existing storefront traffic: thousands of net-new tagged subscribers per year on the existing visitor base. Downstream nurture lift across the segmented list: mid five figures to low six figures incremental on Year-1 sequenced offers. Compounds for the life of the list.

AG1 service. Question logic. Scoring algorithm. Routing rules. Bottleneck readouts. Email-system tagging.

Bottleneck 3 - No application-gated cohort above the all-access membership

What is observable. The Detailers Growth Academy is the highest-tier digital SKU and is sold open-enrolment via Circle checkout. The only seat-capped offer is the $1,200 / 4-seat 2-day Houston in-person class - a training class, not a cohort with a multi-week runway. There is no public-surface cohort that sits above the all-access membership at a higher price band with a 60-90 day implementation runway.

Why it matters. The audience supports a tier above $20K/mo coaching. The R3 shop and the 15-year Oscar Gil operator-credibility surface support an in-person component at a mid-five-figure cycle price. The closest comp in the detailing-coaching ring (Renny Doyle's multi-day in-person ladder) proves the willingness-to-pay at the operator-discipline tier exists. Detail Groove leaves that slot unfilled.

The fix. The Detailer Operator Cohort - application-gated via Typeform, 8-12 operators per cycle, 8-12 week implementation runway, 2 days in-person at the R3 shop in Houston plus weekly group call after, capped pricing in the mid-tier mentorship band above the all-access membership. The application is the filter. The cap is the moat. The in-person at the R3 shop is the differentiator from every digital-only tier in the existing ladder.

Year-1 floor math. Four cycles per year at 8-12 operators per cycle at mid-tier mentorship pricing. Direct revenue floor in the mid-six figures Year 1. The cohort is the highest-margin line item in the Detail Groove catalog once it exists.

AG1 service. Cohort landing copy. Typeform application logic. Scorecard-to-cohort routing. Post-application email sequence. Alumni surface.

Bottleneck 4 - No buyer-tier segmented welcome flow underneath the existing course catalog

What is observable. The 6+ course SKUs plus the all-access membership all route into the same Teachable / Circle / SamCart checkout backend. No public-surface evidence that the welcome sequence forks by purchased tier. The $1,200 in-person buyer and the $297 social-media-course buyer get the same downstream nurture.

Why it matters. The all-access Detailers Growth Academy buyer is the warmest lead in the catalog for the next-tier cohort, but the nurture sequence does not surface a cohort invite at day 30. The $297 single-course buyer is the warmest lead for the all-access upgrade, but the sequence does not surface that upgrade with the right cadence. Every buyer ladder above their own tier is invisible.

The fix. Webhook handler on the Teachable / Circle / SamCart purchase events. Tag every buyer by product (Social Media For Detailers / SEO For Detailers / Detailing as a Business / Detailers Growth Academy all-access / 2-Day In-Person). Fork the welcome sequence per tier. Day 30: single-course buyers get a soft all-access upsell, all-access buyers get the cohort application invite, in-person buyers get the cohort application invite within 14 days of class completion.

Year-1 floor math. Existing buyer base upgrade capture: net-new mid five figures to low six figures Year-1 lift, conservative. The asset compounds against every future buyer that enters the catalog.

AG1 service. Webhook handler. Email-system tag map. Per-tier welcome templates. Day-30 cohort-application invite logic. Content-origin tagging for the lead magnet PDF opt-in.

Stacked Year-1 floor

The four installs stack against the existing storefront, the YouTube channel, the IG audience, the podcast, the FB group, and the R3 sister-shop traffic. Conservative Year-1 floor on the four installs combined: low to mid six figures incremental, weighted heavily on the cohort line item (Bottleneck 3) and the segmented buyer-tier nurture (Bottleneck 4). Two of the four installs (the VSL and the scorecard) compound for the life of the storefront. Two of the four installs (the cohort and the segmented welcome) compound for the life of the buyer base.

What AG1 does not touch

  • The Detailers Growth Academy curriculum itself. Oscar owns the teaching IP. AG1 builds the funnel above it.
  • The podcast (The Detailing Business Class). Live and active. The strategy is to route podcast listeners into the scorecard, not to change the podcast.
  • The R3 Auto Detailing operating shop. That is the credibility anchor. AG1 stays out of the detailing operations.
  • The 2-Day In-Person training class structure. The class stays as-is. The cohort sits above it as a separate tier.
  • The Google Ads + SEO Services line. Adjacent service lines, not in the patch scope.

Install timeline

Less than 14 days from kickoff for all 4 pieces. VSL film day is the only required time from Oscar. The rest is parallel-built. Day-by-day map lives in funnel-blueprint.

Disclaimer

The above floor math is conservative directional estimates based on visible storefront surface, audience size, and comparable cohort-tier comps in the detailing-coaching ring. Outcomes vary by operator inputs (offer fit, scorecard accuracy, cohort delivery quality, list health). The cohort is operator-discipline coaching, not a contractor license, business license, or income guarantee. Standard operator-coaching disclaimers apply across every surface.
