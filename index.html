The Four Pieces

Four installs. Sit on top of the existing storefront.

No course rebuild. No re-platform. No podcast change. The four pieces patch the gaps between the audience, the buy decision, and the existing buyer base.

Piece 01 · Highest-Leverage Pre-Sell
01

The 5-7 minute VSL on detailgroove.co + the Detailers Growth Academy page.

The detailgroove.co homepage opens on a lead magnet PDF opt-in. No homepage video. The mass YouTube and Instagram audience trusts the on-camera operator-educator voice and hits a static product card and a PDF promise on the storefront. The fix is a 5-7 minute VSL filmed at the R3 shop in Oscar's voice, embedded above the lead magnet opt-in, with a mirror embed on the Detailers Growth Academy page. The script walks the 4-band ladder ($0 -> $1K -> $5K -> $20K), pre-sells the all-access, and routes too-early viewers to the lead magnet, ready buyers to the right course tier.

We build the 5-7 minute VSL that pre-sells the right tier for the right buyer. Script, direct the on-shop record, edit the cut, embed on both surfaces, set captions. The voice on screen is Oscar's.

Compounds against every YouTube end card, every IG link-in-bio click, every podcast show-notes click, every X promo click.

Piece 02 · The Qualification Step
02

The 90-second Detailer Readiness Scorecard between the visitor and the catalog.

Six course cards sit side by side on /courses with no filter. SEO For Detailers. Detailing as a Business. Social Media For Detailers. No Talk Just Action. Smoke Odor Removal. Six Figure Email Templates. Plus the Detailers Growth Academy all-access. A new visitor cannot self-identify which SKU matches their revenue band, their solo-vs-team stage, or their primary bottleneck. The fix is a 10-question scorecard that returns Just Starting / Building To $1K / Building To $5K / Building To $20K plus a 3-line bottleneck readout. Captures email. Tags by service line, revenue band, employee count, and primary bottleneck. The scorecard is the upstream router.

We install the "Where Are You In Your Detailing Business?" scorecard that filters before checkout. Question logic, scoring algorithm, routing rules, bottleneck readouts, email tagging. Recommendations route through Oscar's existing catalog.

Filters fit-mismatched buyers out of the wrong course, lifts course-completion rates downstream, captures email at scale across the existing storefront traffic for the first time.

Piece 03 · The Application-Gated Cohort
03

The Detailer Operator Cohort - the unfilled slot above the Detailers Growth Academy all-access.

The Detailers Growth Academy is the highest-tier digital SKU and sells open-enrolment via Circle checkout. The only seat-capped offer is the 2-day Houston in-person class - a training class, not a cohort with an implementation runway. The slot above the all-access at a mid-tier mentorship price band with a 60-90 day runway is unfilled. The audience supports the tier. The R3 shop supports the in-person component. Format: 8-12 operators per cycle, application-gated via Typeform, mid-tier mentorship pricing above the Detailers Growth Academy line, 2 days in-person at the R3 shop plus an 8-12 week implementation runway after. The application is the filter. The cap is the moat. The R3 room is the differentiator from every digital-only tier in the ladder.

We build the application and landing for the Detailer Operator Cohort. Cohort landing copy, Typeform application, scorecard-to-cohort routing, post-application email sequence, alumni surface. Oscar runs the cohort.

Operator-discipline coaching framing across every surface. The in-person room at R3 is the differentiator. Cohort sits on top of the existing ladder, the all-access membership stays.

Piece 04 · The Segmented Welcome Flow
04

The buyer-tier segmentation underneath the existing course catalog.

Single-course buyers, Detailers Growth Academy all-access buyers, and 2-Day In-Person buyers all route into the same downstream. No public-surface evidence of buyer-tier segmentation on the Teachable / Circle / SamCart purchase events. The $1,200 in-person buyer gets the same onboarding nurture as the single-course buyer. The fix is a webhook handler that tags by SKU and forks the welcome sequence per tier. Day 30 - the single-course buyer gets a soft all-access upsell, the all-access buyer gets the cohort application invite, the in-person buyer gets the cohort application invite within 14 days of class completion.

We wire the segmented welcome flow that tags single-course buyers, all-access members, and in-person attendees into different downstream cadences. Webhook handler, email-system tags, welcome templates per tier, content-origin tagging for the lead magnet PDF opt-in.

Captures the upgrade path the existing buyer base does not currently have. Builds the warmest possible audience for the cohort and for any future paid-media spend.

The Install

Less than 14 days from kickoff.

Oscar's only required time is the VSL film day at the R3 shop plus the cohort first-cycle confirmation. The rest is parallel-built.

Day 1-2

Brand colour lock. Voice calibration off a 30-min Oscar transcript. Scorecard questions drafted. Cohort name + price band locked. Welcome-flow tier map.

Day 3

VSL film day at the R3 shop. Scorecard live in staging. Cohort landing copy v1. Per-tier welcome templates drafted.

Day 4-5

VSL edit. Scorecard QA across the 4 bands. Cohort Typeform built. Webhook handler tested end-to-end against a sandbox purchase event.

Day 6-7

VSL embeds live on detailgroove.co + the all-access page. Scorecard public. Cohort Typeform public. YouTube end cards + IG bio + podcast show-notes boilerplate updated.

Day 8-10

Cohort launch trio filmed (1 YouTube + 2 podcast episodes + 5 reel cuts). Per-tier welcome sequences loaded. Post-cohort-application sequence loaded.

Day 11-12

End-to-end test from YouTube click through scorecard, through course purchase, through segmented welcome. Cohort first-cycle date locked.

Day 13-14

Buffer + live-fire test. Cycle 1 of the cohort opens for application.

A Note On Tone

Operator-discipline register, every surface.

The detailing-business-coaching ring is full of income-claim language. The Detail Groove voice on YouTube and the podcast has stayed on the operator side of that line for 15 years. Every public surface in this package keeps that register. No income guarantees. No "make $X with me" framing. The cohort sells operator development at the R3 desk, not signals or returns. The scorecard filters fit-mismatched buyers before checkout, which lifts course-completion rates at the source. The AG1-built surfaces sit underneath Oscar's existing audience-facing register, complementing the brand, not replacing it.
