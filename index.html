The situation

The audience is there. The infrastructure between courses and coaching is not.

Detail Groove has built one of the most trusted detailing business brands online. 252K subscribers. 79.85M views. An active podcast. A proven $1,200 in-person workshop. A high-ticket coaching tier. The free content works.

The problem is in the middle. Between the $497 course ceiling and the $20K/mo coaching floor, there is a $2K-$3K gap where most of the audience lives right now. Operators doing $5K-$15K/mo who are past what YouTube can do for them and not yet ready for 1:1. That gap is where the money is sitting.

There are five places this matters. Each one has a number attached to it. Combined Year-1 conservative floor: $240K-$570K on top of existing revenue.

Five bottlenecks

Where the revenue is sitting right now

Y1 Floor: $40K-$100K
01

252K subscribers converting through a flat PDF opt-in

The homepage primary CTA is a static 10,000-word guide. It captures interest but does not sort leads by business stage. A detailer doing $2K/mo and a detailer doing $15K/mo both get the same download and the same follow-up. The segmentation that would route the $15K/mo operator to the workshop or the cohort is not there.

What is missing

A 90-second business-readiness quiz ("Where is your detailing business right now?") that scores the lead on current monthly revenue, headcount, and client acquisition method, then routes them to the right next product. At 3-5% YouTube-to-email conversion on the existing view volume, that is 2,400 to 4,000+ new segmented leads per year. Workshop waitlists fill from this list. Cohort presells from this list.

Year-1 estimate from list-driven product routing: $40K-$100K incremental.

Y1 Floor: $80K-$250K — Largest gap
02

$497 course ceiling to $20K/mo coaching with no mid-tier bridge

The offer stack has a steep cliff. Courses at $97-$497 on Teachable. The $1,200 in-person workshop capped at four students per class, Houston only. Then the $20K/mo coaching tier. Between the course and the coaching, there is a $2,500 gap where 80% of the engaged audience lives.

What is missing

A productized online cohort at $2K-$3K. 25-30 seats. 8 weeks. Weekly group call. Curriculum: client acquisition, pricing for profitability, systems and SOPs, first hire. This is the online equivalent of the Houston workshop, with no geography constraint and four times the seat capacity. 25 seats x $2,500 x 4 cohorts per year = $250K direct. Cohort graduates become the natural pipeline for high-ticket coaching, arriving pre-vetted and already results-partial.

Year-1 estimate from cohort launch alone: $80K-$250K.

Y1 Floor: $40K-$80K
03

Multi-channel content with no unified front door to the offer stack

Content lives on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and the podcast. Each platform has its own audience that may not know the full offer stack exists. A $15K/mo operator watching YouTube for business tips may not know there is a $1,200 in-person workshop or a high-ticket coaching program. The site navigation does not present a logical progression from "I just started" to "I am ready for coaching."

What is missing

A single "Start Here" pathway page that presents the customer journey in plain language. "Just starting out?" Free guide and starter course. "Doing $3K-$10K/mo and stuck?" Online cohort. "Ready to go all-in?" In-person workshop or apply for coaching. This is not a redesign. It is one page that routes the existing audience to the right tier instead of letting them self-select randomly. Conservative lift on existing course revenue: 15-25%.

Year-1 estimate from routing existing traffic correctly: $40K-$80K incremental.

Y1 Floor: $20K-$60K
04

Podcast with no end-card upsell into cohort or coaching

The Detailing Business Class runs as a standalone brand asset. Episodes teach business principles for detailers. Podcast listeners are the highest-intent free audience segment in the stack. They sat through 20-40 minutes of content, not a 60-second video. The natural end-card that would drive them to the cohort waitlist is not there.

What is missing

A 45-second podcast outro on each episode: "If you are past the free content and want the install version of what we talk about on this show, the cohort waitlist is open. 25 seats. Group calls weekly. This is where the conversations from this podcast become actual changes in your business." Plus a one-page "Podcast to Path" landing that catches podcast-specific traffic. Converting 1% of active listeners to a $2,500 cohort pays on the first episode.

Year-1 estimate from podcast-to-cohort routing: $20K-$60K.

Y1 Floor: $60K-$80K
05

In-person workshop capped at 4 students and geo-locked to Houston

The $1,200 in-person workshop is a strong product. The bottleneck is structural. Four students max per class, held at R3 Auto Detailing in Houston. An operator in Atlanta, London, or Calgary cannot attend. The format does not scale without a physical replica in each city.

What is missing

A "Workshop Vault" that delivers the workshop curriculum digitally: pre-recorded technique modules, live Q&A sessions, operator-specific implementation calls. Priced at $800-$1,000, below the in-person tier, with an "Upgrade to In-Person" upsell. Or a certification path that licenses the curriculum to regional trainers, each running their own four-person workshop. Online intensive: $800 x 50 students = $40K direct. Licensing: $500-$1,000 per cohort x 10 trainers x 4 cohorts = $20K-$40K/year.

Year-1 estimate from unlocking the workshop beyond Houston: $60K-$80K.
