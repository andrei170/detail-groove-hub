9. Buy-Cycle Friction (observable)

  1. Visitor lands on detailgroove.co -> lead magnet opt-in is the primary CTA. The mass YouTube audience routes into a PDF, not a structured funnel.
  2. Visitor goes to /courses -> 6+ SKUs side by side with no "which one for me" filter. Choice paralysis hits before checkout.
  3. Visitor goes to /detailers-growth-academy/ -> all-access membership pitched, price hidden, direct-to-checkout via Circle.
  4. Visitor goes to /detail-training/ -> $1,200 / 2-day / Houston / 4-seat cap. The most premium digital + in-person tier. No public-surface cohort that sits above this with a 60-90 day implementation runway.
  5. The mass-following voice on YouTube does not pre-sell the buy. Visitors who arrived from the channel see a different brand surface on the storefront. The voice gap is observable.
