Foundation

Detail Groove | Copy Strategy

Foundation doc. Voice, brand pillars, headline bank, framing rules for every AG1 surface built on top of the Detail Groove funnel.

Prepared: 2026-05-17 Pitch type: Cold first-touch

1. Voice register

Element Rule
Speaker AG1, operator-funnel-architect register. NOT Oscar's voice. NOT R3 shop voice.
Posture Operator-to-operator. Audience-vs-funnel disparity is the through-line.
Tone No-hype. Operating-shop owner addressing operating-shop owner. The work talks.
Density 1 hero claim per surface. 4 supporting claims max. Density beats adjective stacks.
Cadence Short sentences. Hard stops. No long subordinate clauses.
Adjective load At most 1 adjective per noun. No triple stacks ("bold, fresh, dynamic").

2. Anchor frame for every surface

The audience-vs-funnel disparity. Roughly 249K YouTube subscribers, ~83K Instagram followers, a 15-year operating shop in Houston, a 6-course catalog, an in-person training, and a 2020-active podcast. The funnel underneath is a lead magnet PDF, six course cards side by side, and a 4-seat in-person training. AG1 installs the four pieces that sit on top: pre-sell VSL, readiness scorecard, application-gated cohort above the all-access, and buyer-tier segmented welcome flow.

3. Niche-bans (never appear in any surface)

Word / phrase Reason
"Transform your detailing business" Banned AI phrase.
"Unlock $20K/mo" Banned AI phrase.
"Game-changer" Banned AI phrase.
"Revolutionary" Banned AI phrase.
"Level up" Banned AI phrase.
"Leverage" AG1 banned per voice doc.
"Took a fresh pass" Agency-jargon. Banned per outreach feedback.
"It's not just X, but Y" AI structural tell. Banned.
Triple adjective stacks Banned.
Em-dash (U+2014) ZERO em-dashes. Only literal "- Andrei" sign-off uses hyphen.
"Just" + price Never frame price as "just." Pricing hints only on the landing surface.
"$" anywhere on landing-page.html ZERO dollar signs on the landing surface. Hint language only.
Income guarantees Banned across every surface. Coaching is operator-discipline.
"I'll do for you" without scope Vague. Always tie to a specific install (VSL, scorecard, cohort, welcome flow).
